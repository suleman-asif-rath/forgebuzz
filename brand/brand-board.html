04

Voice & caption

Punchy, curious, never cynical. Same four-part structure on every post so the feed feels like one voice.

💡 The Moon has water on its sunlit side.

Scientists just confirmed water molecules on the Moon's daytime surface — not hidden in shadow, but out in full sunlight. It changes the math for a future lunar base.

Follow @forgebuzz so you always know it first.

#forgebuzz #trending #didyouknow #space #moon #nasa #science #astronomy #world #spacefacts #discovery #forgebuzznews

  • 1Hook — one punchy line that matches the card headline. One emoji, max.
  • 2Context — one or two sentences with the surprising detail. Facts only.
  • 3CTA — always ask for a reaction: follow, comment, or tag a friend.
  • 4Hashtags — 12 total: 3 fixed (#forgebuzz #trending #didyouknow) + topical.
05

Post templates

Three layouts, one identity. Spark-Tile logo top-left, category pill top-right, Anton headline bottom-left, @forgebuzz watermark centred. The renderer fills these from live trends — the frame never changes, so every post is unmistakably ForgeBuzz.
